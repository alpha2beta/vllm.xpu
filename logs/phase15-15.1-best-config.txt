## 15.1.2 Selection (priority: 1) 0 errors/10+ reqs 2) max decode 3) ≥2.5 GiB 4) <60s start)
- SELECTED: **G — MTP K=2, MXFP4 draft, eager, util 0.68, MAX_LEN 4096, KV 0.5GiB.**
  Rationale: highest decode by clear margin (+9% over F, +45% over A); 0 errors
  across all runs; startup ~60s (criterion 4 ~met); criterion 3 unmet in this
  climate (0.9) but environmental — quiet-box MTP history shows 2.1, and no
  config with headroom comes within 30% of the throughput.
- RUNNING: 10-request qualification (golden suite x2) on `tiel-prod-qual` — see below.
- Fallback: F (K=1) if K=2 acceptance ever degrades on a workload (−9% speed,
  same memory, larger KV 6301 vs 5120).
- Rejected: B, C, H (memory/startup), I (no gain), D (superseded).
