## 13.1.1 Attention fraction (from existing results.csv, no profiler needed)
- Short P=1024: 19.15 tok/s -> 52.22 ms/step.
- Long P=6660: 18.81 tok/s -> 53.16 ms/step.

  19.15/19.51/18.81). Decode is MoE-weight-traffic-bound.

  multiple workgroups per seq (splits_per_seq, work_list of
  seq/kv_tile_start/kv_tile_count/split_idx). Heuristic targets ~2x
  oversubscription of Xe cores per KV head, min 4 tiles/WG, cap num_kv_splits=32.

  split mechanism itself exists — 16 query heads do NOT leave 48/64 EUs idle at
  long context; work scales with KV tiles.
