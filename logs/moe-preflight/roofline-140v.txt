## Notes
- copy_gbps counts read+write (2 * bytes / time).
- GoW/s = 10^9 original (post-dequant) weights per second.
- ESIMD is the production fast path; Triton is the K-generic reference/fallback. Both are measured here so the gap is visible.
- K=3/mcg decode requires the (K=3, cb=1) ESIMD instantiation; on a build without it the linear rows report esimd_path=false.
